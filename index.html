Large Net Mastery Planner

Paste your FarmRPG mastery page and see how many Grand and Mega Masteries your Large Nets can buy, and where to throw them.

Copy your mastery page

✓

In FarmRPG, go to Home and scroll down near the bottom of the page. Under "Perks, Mastery & More," click Mastery Progress.

Expand every dropdown first: Tier V, IV, III, II, I, No Tier, and Mega Mastered. Collapsed tiers don't get copied.

Your paste or file stays on your device. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

Each number is how many masteries you'd finish by spending that column's nets at that row's fishing spot.

Every item, fewest nets first

Nets are counted at the best spot for each item. Type in the Caught column to fix or fill in a number.

Drop rates from Buddy's Almanac (snapshot, September 2026). Large Nets catch 250 items, 400 with Reinforced Netting, and 500 with the Fishing Trawl too. Results are averages, so real hauls will wobble a bit. Unofficial fan tool.
